Privacy Policy

Updated 12 October 2026. The short version: your video stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your videos, photos and sound are stored in your browser on the device you use and are never sent to us. Captions are written on your device. Beats are generated on your device. Exports are rendered on your device.

If you sign in

  • Your account. With a wallet, we store your public wallet address. With Google, we store the name, email address and profile picture Google shares with us, and nothing else from your Google account. With email, we store your email address. Accounts are kept by our database provider, Supabase.
  • Your projects and setup. So you can open them on another device, we save each project's edit (clip names, times, text, settings, not the media itself) and your setup answers (name, what you make, where you post) to your account.
  • Your plan. We store your plan, when it ends, and the orders you made.

Payments are public

Payments happen on the Base blockchain. Anyone can see a blockchain transfer, including the sending wallet, the amount and the time. We link each payment to your account so your plan can switch on. We never hold your funds or your keys.

What leaves your device

  • Topi questions. When you ask Topi something, your message, your setup answers (name, what you make, where you post) and a text summary of the project (clip names, lengths, text on screen) go to our server and on to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce the answer. No video, audio or images are sent. We count requests per day and keep no transcript.
  • Learning your style. Topi counts which tools, looks and styles you use, on your device, and sends a short summary with your questions so its answers fit how you edit.
  • Downloads. The first time you use the clip tool or auto captions, the editing engine (about 31 MB) and the caption model (about 40 MB) are fetched from a content delivery network and cached in your browser.
  • Fonts. Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Google sees the request like any web font.
  • Hosting logs. Our host, Vercel, keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, time, page) for a short time.

No tracking

There are no analytics scripts, no advertising pixels and no cookies for tracking. We may add simple, anonymous usage counts later and will say so here.

Email

If you tick the optional box, we will email product news. Reply "stop" to any email to end it. We never sell or share addresses.

Deleting your data

Delete a project from the home page, or clear this site's data in your browser to remove what is on your device. If you signed in, "Delete my account and data" in your account removes your account, projects, setup and usage from our side. Payment records on the blockchain are public and permanent, and we cannot delete those.

Children

Topier is for people 13 and older, 16 in the EU.
